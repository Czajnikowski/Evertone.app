Support

Evertone runs on Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later.

How do I play a note?

Tap a pad. The screen shows one octave, twelve semitones in three columns: from C at the top of the first column to B at the bottom of the last.

How do I change the octave?

Swipe sideways. A quick flick moves a whole octave; a slow drag stops at any column. Pulling up from the bottom of the screen returns to the last note you played, or to octave 5 if you haven’t played one yet.

How do I change the instrument?

Tap the instrument name next to the clock and choose Piano, Strings, Brass or Voice.

How do I set the volume?

Turn the Digital Crown. The volume is kept until the next launch.

How does Whisper work?

Hold a pad until it pulses. Raise the watch to your ear and double tap the fingers of the hand wearing it. The note plays at your ear. Tapping the pulsing pad plays it too; holding it or tapping anywhere else cancels.

Double tap is available on Apple Watch Series 9, Ultra 2 and later. On older watches, Evertone first learns the move: “Hand on your shoulder, by the neck”, three times, with your hand on the opposite shoulder. After that it plays when it recognises the move.

There’s no sound

Turn up the volume with the crown: at its lowest setting the sound is barely audible. Also check the system volume in the watch’s Settings (Sounds & Haptics). If Bluetooth headphones are connected to the watch, the sound may be playing there.

What tuning does it use?

Equal temperament with A5 = 440 Hz. Octave 5 is the one starting at middle C.

Something not working, or an idea? Write to listen@evertone.app

Include your watch model and watchOS version; it helps find the cause sooner.
