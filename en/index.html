For Apple Watch

A tone always at hand

Twelve pads on your wrist. Tap one and the note sounds right away, with no phone, no tuning fork and no piano to look for.

Coming soonto the App Store Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later

The note, the moment you need it

Your phone is in your bag, the tuning fork has gone missing and there’s no piano nearby. Your watch is always on your wrist. Evertone turns it into a clean tone, ready in a second.

  • Tuning

    Guitar, violin, ukulele: play an A and tune by ear. Equal temperament, A = 440 Hz, or whatever pitch your ensemble tunes to.

  • A pitch for the choir

    Give the starting note before a rehearsal or a performance, in one move, without reaching into a pocket.

  • A note to sing

    Train your ear, warm up your voice, or check how an interval sounds. Every note across six octaves is under your finger.

Only in Evertone

Whisper: a note just for you

Sometimes nobody else should hear the note: in a choir before your entry, on stage, in a hall. Whisper plays it only once the watch is at your ear, for you alone.

  1. Hold a pad

    A long press sets the note aside for later. Its pad pulses while it waits.

  2. Raise the watch to your ear

    Nothing plays until the watch is in place.

  3. Double tap your fingers

    The double tap gesture plays the note right at your ear. Tap again to repeat it.

On watches without the double tap gesture, Evertone learns how you raise your hand to your ear and plays when it recognises the move.

What it does

  • Four instrumentsPiano, Strings, Brass and Voice. Tap the name next to the clock to switch.
  • Six octavesSwipe sideways, one octave at a time. Pull up to return to the last note.
  • Twelve pads per octaveEvery semitone at once, large and easy to hit.
  • Tuning from 415 to 466 HzA = 440 Hz by default. For an orchestra, early music or a band that tunes differently, set A with the crown, 1 Hz at a time, and hear it right away.
  • Volume on the crownThe Digital Crown sets the volume, finely at quiet levels. Your watch remembers it.
  • Plays instantlySound synthesised on the watch, with no delay and no internet.
  • For everyoneVoiceOver, larger text, increased contrast, reduced motion and differentiate without colour. Seventeen languages.

Collects no data

No account, no ads, no analytics. Evertone never connects to the internet, and your settings stay on your watch. Privacy policy
